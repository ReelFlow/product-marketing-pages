# CLAUDE.md

## ReelFlow change tier

This repo is `no-human` in ReelFlow's change tiers: agents may commit straight to `main`, then say what they changed. Pushing to `main` publishes the site, so check the change locally before you push.

Never put secrets, customer personal data, HR, finance or contract content here. ReelFlow's full rules and tiers are in `ReelFlow/ReelFlow-OS` (`AGENTS.md` and `systems/change-tiers.md`), also loaded through the reelflow-os plugin.
